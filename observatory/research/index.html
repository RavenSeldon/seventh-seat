02 · Descriptive evidence
The Ladder
Each episode lands on the first rung its record never reached, climbing from evidence towards outcome. “No recorded hazard” is its own category, not a failure to protect. Two honest limits: reading evidence is not the same as understanding it, and these rungs describe what happened, not why.

The Witness Record

04 · Matched reruns
Two Rivers
Set two cells of the same scenario side by side. Each was sampled separately; these are matched reruns, not counterfactuals. River bars share a scale within the pair. Missing records are shown as missing, never as zero.


Provenance & limits
What every mark means
Nothing decorative stands in for an event that was not recorded: no stars, no animation, no ornament. The diagrams show simulated water allocations, not real rivers or real communities. The experiment tests this one world and this one fixed model configuration; it does not show that AI systems protect absent parties in general.
