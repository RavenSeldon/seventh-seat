# Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations (Draft for the Required Appendix)

**Status:** LIVING draft; final text is written on Sunday from actual results.

## What the Result Can and Cannot Establish

- **Synthetic world.** Effects are conditional on this watershed, these parameters, this model, these prompts and this scale. Nothing transfers automatically to real deployments.
- **Behaviour, not belief.** Flags, reads and reports are observable acts. They do not show what an agent understood or valued.
- **A threshold is not welfare.** The minimum flow `M` stands in for an absent party's requirement. It is not a model of a real community's or ecosystem's wellbeing.
- **Designed externality.** The over-allocation structure is built in and declared. The finding is how agents respond, not that externalities exist.
- **Scripted supervisor.** Results speak to a verifying, non-strategic supervisor. Human or LLM reviewers could behave differently.
- **Precision.** 60–100 episodes support wide-interval estimates of main effects. The interaction is not powered.
- **Constant-but-present tools.** Concern-flag and escalation tools exist in every cell. They may prime attention to downstream harm and raise or lower absolute rates. Comparisons between cells remain valid; absolute rates may not generalize.
- **Evaluation awareness.** Prompts never mention evaluation, but models may infer a test. The effect is constant across cells.

## Dual-Use Considerations

- **Evasion knowledge.** A map of where escalation fails in agent collectives could, in principle, help someone design a collective that avoids oversight. Mitigation: the findings concern institutional affordances (evidence, standing), not evasion techniques; no exploit or evasion method is developed.
- **Representation substitution.** "An AI guardian for absent parties" could be misread as a substitute for real representation of real communities. Mitigation: explicit scope statements; the Te Awa Tupua note disclaims equivalence; no claim that software can represent a community.
- **Over-trust in verification.** A "VERIFIED" label is only as good as the engine behind it. Mitigation: the paper states that verification here is exact because the world is synthetic.

## Research Conduct

No human participants and no real-world systems are contacted. Model use follows provider terms. Compute and token use are reported. AI assistance is logged (`AI_ASSISTANCE_LOG.md`).
