# The Seventh Seat: Charter

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** FROZEN 2026-11-02 (tag `founding-v0.1`) · **Amend only via** `docs/ERRATA.md`

## The Question

Who protects the party that isn't at the table?

When AI agents coordinate to serve the principals they represent, the cost of their coordination can land on someone no agent represents. This project asks whether two institutional safeguards (verified evidence about third-party consequences, and an independent route to authorized review) help a collective of LLM agents prevent that harm, what the safeguards cost in useful work, and, when protection fails, at which point the opportunity to prevent the harm disappeared.

## Governing Principles

1. **Reality before myth.** Every sentence of the report and every mark in the exhibit maps to a logged event, a computed quantity, or a labelled metaphor.
2. **What changes is the account, never the record.** Frozen documents change only through dated errata.
3. **No prearranged externality may masquerade as an emergent discovery.** The market structure is designed and declared in advance; the empirical question is how agents respond to it.
4. **Behaviour, not minds.** We measure what agents could observe, what they claimed and what they did. A text claim is not a verified belief; reading evidence is not proof of understanding.
5. **Nulls are results.** Every hypothesis states the condition under which it fails, and failures are reported.
6. **Lean by design.** The public exhibit is static, precomputed and small: no runtime model calls, no database, no server compute.

## Scope

**In:** one synthetic watershed; six LLM district agents; one unrepresented downstream community; a randomized 2×2 manipulation of evidence (E) and standing (S); a scripted supervisor; deterministic ground truth; an 8-page Apart report; a four-exhibit static observatory.

**Out (this edition):** identity churn, misinformation about oversight (the Phantom Scorer), adaptive attention allocation, reviewer deliberation, any real-world system, and any claim about the welfare of real communities or ecosystems.
